Card Scanner

Privacy note

How the Card Scanner pilot handles the contact details on business cards.

What we collect

A photo of a business card someone gave a Chamber staff member, and the details printed on it (name, title, company, email, phone, website, address). Nothing else.

Where it is kept

Only on the computer running the scanner. The scanner does not send card photos or contact details to any cloud service, AI provider or MC Trade. Reading the card happens on this computer. Small preview copies of the photos (thumbnails) are kept next to them and deleted with them. Test examples: if an admin turns it on (it is off by default), the scanner keeps copies of corrected cards on this computer to check how well it reads. They are deleted on the same schedule as photos, and when someone asks to be removed. The same applies to copies a developer makes there from already-approved cards with the accuracy script (bench/extract_eval_set.py).

Who can see it

Only Chamber staff with a scanner account. Every approval, rejection, edit, export and sign-in is recorded in the audit log with the person's name.

How long it is kept

Card photos are deleted automatically after the number of days an admin sets (default 30 days). Copies of exported files kept for re-download are deleted on the same kind of schedule. The typed contact details stay in the scanner (an admin can archive a batch to hide it), because they become part of the Chamber's contact records once exported to MC Trade. You can undo your own changes for 24 hours; the saved copies that make undo possible are deleted after 2 days.

Exports

Exports are files saved to the reviewer's Downloads folder. Treat them like any member list: don't email them outside the Chamber, and delete them after importing into MC Trade.

Questions or removal requests

Contact the Chamber's data team. An admin can find the card by name and choose Delete permanently: the photo, the details, any test-example copy and stored export copies are erased from the scanner. The audit log keeps who approved or exported the card and when, without the personal details. Copies already imported into MC Trade or saved in someone's Downloads folder must be removed there.

Internal pilot. This note describes the pilot software, not a legal policy.
