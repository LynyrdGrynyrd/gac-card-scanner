    AI double-check

    Cards are read quickly first. If a card looks uncertain, a slower local AI model (running on this computer only) double-checks it in the background and suggests corrections to names, companies, titles and addresses; nothing changes until a person accepts a suggestion. Cards a person has already edited or decided are never changed.

    Privacy & retention

    Card photos are only needed while a card is being reviewed. Deleting them after a set time limits what is stored. The typed contact details are kept.

    Read the privacy note

    The check also runs automatically every hour while the scanner is running.

    Pilot test data

    Archive every card currently in the queue (all statuses), for example after a training session. Archived cards are hidden from the queue and exports but kept in the database.
